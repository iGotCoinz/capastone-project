NIL Compliance & Financial FAQ

Do NIL earnings count as taxable income?

Yes, NIL earnings are considered taxable income and must be reported.

Can my school help manage my NIL deals?

Some schools offer advisors, but you are ultimately responsible.

What about free products instead of cash?

Non-cash NIL compensation may still be taxable based on market value.
