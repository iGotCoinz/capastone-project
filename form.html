NIL Compliance & Financial FAQ

Are NIL earnings taxable?

Yes, NIL earnings are taxable and must be reported.

Can schools help manage NIL?

Some offer support, but athletes remain responsible.

Are free products taxable?

Non-cash NIL items may be taxed at fair market value.
