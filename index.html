NIL Compliance & Financial FAQ

Do NIL earnings count as taxable income?

Yes. NIL income must be reported on your tax return. Set aside 25–30%.

Can my school help manage my NIL deals?

You are ultimately responsible for understanding your contracts.

What if I'm offered free products instead of cash?

Non-cash NIL compensation can still be taxable.
